SimLearn Lite

Students: your teacher will give you the link to today's simulation.

SimLearn Lite is a simpler version of SimLearn, a set of role-play simulations for university English courses. Each student takes a role in a realistic problem, the group talks its way to a decision, and everyone writes it up.

  • One link, which teachers add to Moodle. No sign-up.
  • No cookies and no tracking. Your work stays in your browser tab until you hand it in.
  • Your writing is only checked with AI if you agree to it.
